I · Academic Profile

Connecting mechanism discovery with pharmacological intervention through vascular biology.

Dr. Haocheng Lu received his Bachelor of Medicine in Clinical Medicine from Peking University Health Science Center and a dual Bachelor's degree in Economics from the National School of Development, Peking University. He earned his Ph.D. in Physiology from the University of Michigan in 2019, followed by postdoctoral training in the Department of Internal Medicine at the University of Michigan with Prof. Y. Eugene Chen. In June 2022, he joined the School of Medicine at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) as an Assistant Professor and Principal Investigator.

Our research centers on aortic aneurysm, atherosclerosis, metabolic dysfunction, and vascular cell homeostasis, bridging human genetics, single-cell multi-omics, in vivo functional genomics, and targeted nanomedicine to translate mechanistic discoveries into therapeutic strategies.

II · Research Pillars

Research Directions

Addressing life-threatening aortic diseases and chronic cardiometabolic vascular injury through three interconnected research pillars.

Vascular Remodeling & Aneurysm

Aortic Aneurysm Mechanisms & Targets

Dissecting how vascular smooth muscle cells, macrophages, perivascular adipose tissue (PVAT), and genetic variants drive aortic dilation and rupture risk.

  • Identified the VSMC TFEB–BCL2 protective axis in AAA and established cyclodextrin-based TFEB activation therapy.
  • Uncovered TCF7L2 regulatory variants and ECM remodeling pathways in thoracic and abdominal aortic aneurysms.
  • Mapped region-specific PVAT remodeling under AngII stimulation.
Aortic Aneurysm TFEB-BCL2 TCF7L2 PVAT Heterogeneity
Atherosclerosis & Immunometabolism

Atherosclerosis & Metabolic Diseases

Investigating how shear stress, endothelial inflammation, and lipid/glucose dysregulation alter vascular homeostasis and systemic metabolism.

  • Demonstrated endothelial TFEB's protective role against vascular inflammation, oxidative stress, and atherosclerosis.
  • Defined the roles of TFEB and caveolae/CAV1 in angiogenesis, endothelial signaling, and systemic metabolic homeostasis.
  • Revealed how hypertriglyceridemia and lipid regulators such as TM6SF2 shape vascular and metabolic disease phenotypes.
Shear Stress Endothelial Inflammation Caveolae / CAV1 Lipid Metabolism
In Vivo Functional Genomics & Pharmacology

Cardiovascular Drug Discovery & Translation

Developing in vivo functional genomics platforms and targeted delivery modalities to bridge high-throughput gene discovery with therapeutic intervention.

  • Built the in vivo macrophage CRISPR screening platform (CRISPR-MI) to pinpoint disease drivers directly within vascular lesions.
  • Developed lipid nanoparticle (LNP)-mediated TFEB mRNA delivery for treating metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD).
  • Evaluated nitro-fatty acids, cyclodextrin, and emodin as pharmacological candidates for vascular and metabolic protection.
CRISPR-MI LNP-mRNA Delivery TREM2 MASLD
